# RasaLore AI

Ubiquitous domain language and business terminology for RasaLore AI, a local-first cultural menu translation and allergen intelligence platform for food and beverage businesses.

## Language

**Cultural Narrative**:
A descriptive, culturally contextualized English portrayal of a traditional dish that explains its culinary heritage and preparation rather than translating its literal name.
_Avoid_: Literal translation, direct translation, word-for-word translation

**Flavor Profile**:
A structured set of sensory taste and aroma attributes that characterize the dish's culinary experience.
_Avoid_: Taste notes, flavor tags, taste description

**Allergen Flag**:
A critical dietary safety indicator highlighting common food allergens or compliance with specific religious and lifestyle diets.
_Avoid_: Food warning, allergy tag, dietary alert

**Side Hustle Margin**:
The quantifiable cost savings and profit margin achieved by running local open-source AI models compared to subscription cloud APIs.
_Avoid_: ROI calculator, profit estimate, token savings

**Dual-Mode Inference**:
An architecture that runs locally offline on an open-weight model engine by default, while providing an online web fallback for remote evaluation.
_Avoid_: Hybrid AI, multi-cloud LLM, mixed runtime
